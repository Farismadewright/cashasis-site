# Evidence and charts
Browse current primary sources for market statistics and law/tax/foreclosure claims. Prefer actual HAR reports, county appraisal/tax offices, official courts/statutes, Census/FRED/source datasets. Read sources, not snippets; verify jurisdiction and effective/reporting dates.
For every claim retain URL, title, access date, publication date, reporting period, geography, unit, exact figure/denominator, methodology and supported sentence. Use latest released data, labeled with its actual period. Distinguish reporting month from publication month, median from mean, list from sold price, metro from county and single-family from all property types. Omit inaccessible/conflicting claims or HOLD; never guess.
Graphs must use retained numeric data and deterministic plotting, not imagegen. Label units, axes, dates, geography, source and methodology; include accessible table. Avoid misleading truncated axes; distinguish observed history, projections and hypothetical examples. Recalculate all examples. Never present association as causation or imply an aggregate statistic describes a particular seller/property.
Use direct source links in article prose; avoid extensive copying. Never use private lead data publicly without authorization.

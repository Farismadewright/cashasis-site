## Build a simple payoff picture before guessing at your proceeds

Start with the latest property-tax statement or notice you have, then add any mortgage payoff, known liens, and expected closing obligations. The exact figures still need to be confirmed through the proper closing process, but this gives the seller a clearer framework: property value minus legitimate payoffs and transaction costs equals the money potentially available after closing. That is more useful than looking only at the headline sale price.

## Harris County seller checklist

This page is specifically for the **Harris County information-gathering step** before a sale. For the broader explanation of how delinquent taxes can affect a Houston sale, use our [Houston back-tax guide](/blog/sell-house-behind-on-taxes-houston/).

Before discussing a closing date or expected proceeds, gather:

1. The property address and the owner name shown on your latest records.
2. The most recent property-tax statement or delinquency notice you have.
3. Any separate notice mentioning a lawsuit, hearing, judgment, sale date, or collection attorney.
4. Your mortgage payoff information, if the property still has a loan.
5. Any known HOA, judgment, estate, probate, or other title issue.
6. The date by which you actually need the property sold.

For current Harris County tax-account information, use the [Harris County Tax Office property-tax resources](https://www.hctax.net/Property/PropertyTax). Exact balances and legal status should be confirmed through the responsible tax/title professionals rather than estimated from this article.

## You inherited the property—not an obligation to keep dealing with it

An inherited property can carry memories.

It can also carry repairs, belongings, taxes, insurance, maintenance, title questions, multiple heirs, and a growing list of things someone has to handle.

**You are allowed to decide that the property has become more responsibility than you want.**

Selling does not erase the memories or the person connected to the house.

Sometimes it simply allows the family to close one chapter and move forward.

## CashAsIs helps take the property work off your plate

If you're ready to be finished with the property, CashAsIs can make the process simpler.

We buy inherited properties **as-is**.

That means you do not have to turn the property into a renovation project before talking to us.

No traditional market preparation first. No staging. No months of trying to make an inherited house perfect for someone else.

## No repairs

If the property is dated or needs work, show it to us that way.

You do not need to remodel the kitchen, replace flooring, paint, landscape, or complete a long repair list before receiving a CashAsIs offer.

We evaluate the property in its current condition.

## No full cleanout first

Take the belongings your family wants to keep.

If there are remaining contents, discuss them with us as part of the transaction.

You do not necessarily need to spend weeks emptying the property before finding out what CashAsIs will pay.

## We help with the closing details

Inherited-property sales can involve more than the physical house.

There may be multiple heirs, an Affidavit of Heirship, probate questions, liens, a mortgage, delinquent property taxes, or other title requirements.

When you sell to CashAsIs, **we work with the title company to identify the documentation and signatures required for the actual closing.**

You don't need to become the title coordinator for the family.

## You can start before everything is solved

One of the biggest mistakes families make is assuming they have to solve every problem before they can even find out what the property is worth to a buyer.

You don't.

CashAsIs can review the property and give you an offer first.

If the number works for your family, we can then move forward with title and determine what must be completed to close.

That gives you something concrete to make a decision around.

## It's okay to move on

Maybe the property has been sitting vacant.

Maybe nobody in the family wants it.

Maybe everyone lives somewhere else.

Maybe the repairs are more than anyone wants to take on.

Or maybe you're simply ready to stop thinking about the property every week.

**That's enough reason to explore a sale.**

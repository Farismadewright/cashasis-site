## One heir can change the closing path

When multiple people may have an ownership interest, a direct buyer cannot simply ignore a person whose signature or legal authority is required. The exact solution depends on how title is held and the estate facts, so this is a situation where title professionals or legal counsel may need to determine who must participate.

CashAsIs can still evaluate the property while those questions are being clarified. That lets the family separate **“what might the property sell for as-is?”** from **“what signatures or estate steps are required to close?”**

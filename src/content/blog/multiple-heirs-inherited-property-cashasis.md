## Multiple heirs doesn't have to mean multiple headaches

Inherited properties often become complicated because the property is only one part of the situation.

There may be siblings, children, a surviving spouse, relatives in different states, questions about a will, or uncertainty about who actually has authority to sell.

**CashAsIs helps simplify that process with the title company.**

Instead of asking your family to figure out the ownership structure before talking to us, we can review the property, make an as-is offer, and work with title to determine what is actually required to close.

**IDENTIFY REQUIRED HEIRS → GET REQUIRED SIGNATURES → CLOSE**

## We help determine who actually needs to sign

One of the biggest sources of confusion is assuming every relative needs to sign—or assuming one family member can sign for everyone.

Neither assumption is always correct.

The required parties depend on factors such as:

- how the deceased owner held title;
- whether there is a surviving spouse;
- whether there is a will;
- whether probate has been opened;
- the deceased owner's family history;
- prior deaths within the family;
- recorded deeds and estate documents; and
- the title company's underwriting requirements.

When you sell to CashAsIs, **we coordinate with title to determine the required ownership and signatures for the actual property.**

You do not have to solve that puzzle by yourself before getting an offer.

## What if some heirs live out of town?

That's common.

Inherited-property families may be spread across Houston, other parts of Texas, or different states entirely.

The title company determines the acceptable signing and closing arrangements for the transaction. Depending on the file, documents may be coordinated with heirs in different locations.

CashAsIs helps keep the property transaction moving while title handles the formal closing requirements.

## What if the family doesn't know all the heirs?

Start with what you know.

Give us the deceased owner's name and the family relationships you are aware of. Title can then review the chain of title and determine what additional heirship information or documentation is needed.

For qualifying estates, an **Affidavit of Heirship** may be one part of establishing the family history for title.

Other estates may require probate or additional legal work.

Our role is to help get the property into a real closing process so the correct requirements can be identified.

## What if the heirs disagree?

CashAsIs cannot make family members agree or provide legal advice in an heir dispute.

But having a **real cash offer** can often give the family something concrete to evaluate.

Instead of debating an unknown property value, everyone can see the actual CashAsIs purchase number and decide whether the sale works for them.

If the required parties agree to proceed, we work with title toward closing.

## No repairs or market preparation first

Multiple heirs are complicated enough without also coordinating contractors, repairs, cleaning, staging, and showings.

CashAsIs buys inherited properties **as-is**.

The family does not need to make the property market-ready before we make an offer.

That can be especially useful when nobody lives near the property or nobody wants responsibility for managing it.

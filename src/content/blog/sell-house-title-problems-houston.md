## Title problems are often discovered when somebody finally tries to sell.

A deceased owner may still appear in records. Names may not match. An old lien may still show. Multiple owners may need to sign. A prior transfer may require additional documentation. These are different problems, so the first useful step is identifying the actual title issue.

That is why we want the real story about the property. The more accurately we understand the situation, the more useful our evaluation can be.

## Do not confuse title work with property renovation

Even if paperwork needs attention, you can still show us the house in its current physical condition. CashAsIs can evaluate the real estate while title professionals determine what must happen for a valid closing.

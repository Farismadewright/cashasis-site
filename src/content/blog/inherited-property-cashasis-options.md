## You inherited the property. You don't have to untangle everything alone.

An inherited property can arrive with more than a set of keys.

There may be old title records, multiple heirs, a mortgage, property taxes, belongings still inside the house, deferred repairs, or uncertainty about whether probate has to be completed before anything can happen.

**CashAsIs is built to help simplify that process.**

Instead of requiring you to solve every title and estate issue before contacting us, we can review the property, make an as-is offer, and work with the title company to determine what is actually required to complete the sale.

## You may not need a full probate administration first

A common assumption is that every inherited property has to spend months going through probate court before it can be sold.

That is not always the case in Texas.

Depending on how the property was owned, whether there was a will, the family history, the heirs, and the title company's requirements, a qualifying estate may have a simpler route for establishing heirship or clearing title.

One tool that may be available is an **Affidavit of Heirship**.

When appropriate and accepted by title, that process may allow the ownership and heirs to be documented without requiring the seller to complete a full probate administration first.

**That can potentially mean less court delay, fewer procedural steps, and lower legal expense than a full probate case.**

Some estates absolutely do require probate or legal representation. We do not promise that an Affidavit of Heirship will work for every property. The point is that **you do not have to assume full probate is required before you talk to us.**

## CashAsIs helps coordinate the closing path

When you choose to sell to CashAsIs, our process starts with the property—not with asking you to become an expert in Texas estate law.

We can help gather the information title needs to evaluate the file, such as:

- the deceased owner's information;
- available death certificate or estate documents;
- known heirs and family relationships;
- an existing will, if there is one;
- mortgage or lien information; and
- property-tax information.

From there, the title company determines what documentation and signatures are required for an insurable closing.

If a simpler heirship route is available, we can work through that closing process with title. If formal probate is required, you'll know that based on the actual file rather than assuming it from the beginning.

## That can save valuable time

Court proceedings can add steps and time to an inherited-property sale.

When the circumstances allow title to clear the property through a simpler process, avoiding an unnecessary full probate administration can help move the transaction toward closing sooner.

That matters when the property is sitting vacant, taxes are accumulating, insurance and utilities continue, or the family simply wants to finish dealing with the property.

## It can also avoid unnecessary expense

Formal probate often involves court procedures and legal representation.

If the estate qualifies for a simpler title solution, there may be an opportunity to avoid some of those court-related and attorney expenses.

CashAsIs does not charge the seller a commission, and our typical direct purchase is structured so the seller is not paying traditional agent commissions or repair costs to prepare the property for us.

The exact title, legal, and estate requirements depend on the specific property.

## You don't have to repair or clean out the property first

Inherited houses are often not ready for a traditional sale.

There may be years of belongings inside. Repairs may have been deferred. The property may be vacant or dated.

That's okay.

**CashAsIs buys properties as-is.**

You can show us the property in its current condition. You do not need to renovate it, stage it, or make it market-ready before we make an offer.

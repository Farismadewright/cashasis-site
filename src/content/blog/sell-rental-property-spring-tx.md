## The short answer

**Yes. A Spring, Texas rental property can be evaluated for a direct sale whether it is occupied or between tenants.**

The useful starting point is the landlord file: lease status, rent, deposit, occupancy, access, recent repairs, and any notices already given. If the house is vacant between tenants, describe the make-ready work it needs. If a tenant remains, be accurate about possession and access.

CashAsIs can evaluate the property without requiring another retail-style turnover first. Tenant rights and lease obligations still matter when applicable. For more local information, see [Sell Your House Fast in Spring](/blog/sell-house-fast-spring-tx/) and [our Houston tenant guide](/blog/sell-house-with-tenants-houston/).

## Gather the landlord file before deciding how to sell

Useful information includes the lease, deposit amount, current rent, payment history, tenant contact details, maintenance requests, recent repairs, utility responsibilities, and any notices already delivered. You do not need a perfect file to start, but the more accurately the occupancy is described, the easier it is to evaluate a realistic transaction. Never promise vacant possession if you do not actually have it.

## Compare another turnover with an as-is exit

A landlord nearing the end of a tenancy may be facing paint, flooring, cleaning, landscaping, appliances, deferred maintenance, leasing costs, and another vacancy period. Before automatically starting that cycle, compare it with a direct as-is evaluation. CashAsIs can consider the Spring property in its current state. Existing tenant rights and lease obligations remain important, but a sale can be explored without first performing a retail-style make-ready for us.

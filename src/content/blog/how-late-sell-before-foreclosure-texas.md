**The practical deadline is before the foreclosure sale, but waiting too long can leave too little time for title, payoff, signatures, and closing.**

## The useful answer is not a fixed number of days

There is no responsible universal answer such as “you can always sell until X days before foreclosure.” The usable window depends on the scheduled sale, lender payoff, title condition, required signatures, and whether a buyer can actually close in time.

That is why CashAsIs wants to know about a known deadline **at the beginning** of the conversation. If the property fits what we buy, a direct cash transaction can remove repair preparation and retail-buyer financing from the process, but title and lender requirements still have to be completed.

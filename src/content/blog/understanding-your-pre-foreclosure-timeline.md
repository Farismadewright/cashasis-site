If your property is in pre-foreclosure, one date matters most: **the scheduled foreclosure sale date.**

If you are considering selling the property, the sooner you know that date and share it with the people handling the transaction, the more time there is to review the property, make an offer, complete title work, obtain payoff information, and close.

## First, confirm where you stand

Contact your mortgage servicer and confirm:

- your scheduled foreclosure sale date, if one has been set;
- the amount needed to reinstate or pay off the loan; and
- whether any foreclosure-prevention option, adjustment, or additional time may be available for your loan.

Options vary by loan and servicer. Do not assume a scheduled sale has been postponed unless your servicer or another authorized party confirms it.

## If you are considering selling, start early

Getting a purchase offer is only the first step. A completed sale can also require title review, mortgage payoff information, signatures, and time to resolve any title or lien issues.

That is why waiting until the final days can make a sale harder to complete.

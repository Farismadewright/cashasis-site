## Don't turn a property you don't want into another project

If you're already tired of dealing with a vacant or unused property, the last thing you may want is a renovation project.

Yet owners often assume they need to fix everything before they can sell.

New paint. Flooring. Landscaping. Trash removal. Contractors. Cleaning. Repairs.

**Not with CashAsIs.**

We buy properties **as-is**.

## Don't fix it

You do not need to renovate the property before we make an offer.

If it has:

- an old roof;
- outdated interiors;
- damaged flooring;
- plumbing or electrical issues;
- deferred maintenance;
- exterior damage;
- code-related conditions; or
- years of wear and tear,

show it to us that way.

CashAsIs evaluates the property in its current condition.

## Don't clean it out first

Vacant properties often accumulate belongings, furniture, debris, old appliances, or items left by previous occupants.

Before you hire a dumpster or spend weekends hauling things away, find out what CashAsIs will pay **as it sits**.

If there are contents you want to keep, take those.

Then tell us what's remaining so the condition and contents can be addressed as part of the transaction.

## Don't make it market-ready

A traditional listing can require work before buyers ever see the property.

That may include cleaning, photography, landscaping, staging, showings, inspections, and repair negotiations.

If you don't even live near the property, coordinating all of that can become a job.

CashAsIs purchases directly.

**No staging. No open houses. No trying to make the property look perfect for our offer.**

## Don't travel back just to manage contractors

Absentee ownership doesn't necessarily mean you live hundreds of miles away.

You might simply live across town and have no reason to keep driving to a neighborhood where you no longer live.

Either way, coordinating contractors, lawn crews, cleanout companies, and repair appointments takes time.

If your goal is to sell the property anyway, ask yourself whether spending more money and time preparing it is actually worth it.

## Start with the CashAsIs number

Before you:

- hire a contractor;
- order a dumpster;
- pay for another cleanup;
- replace anything;
- stage the property; or
- make another trip just to deal with it,

find out what CashAsIs will pay today.

Send us the property address and tell us what condition it's in.

We'll review it and give you a no-obligation offer.

**Don't fix it. Don't clean it. Don't prep it. Let us see the property exactly as it sits.**

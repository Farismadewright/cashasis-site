## Take what you want. Leave the rest.

An inherited property can come with decades of belongings.

Furniture. Boxes. Clothes. Tools. Old appliances. Family photos. Things you want to keep—and plenty of things nobody wants to deal with.

Then there may be repairs on top of it.

**With CashAsIs, you don't have to turn the inherited property into another project.**

We buy inherited properties **as-is**.

## No full cleanout before we make an offer

You do not need to empty the entire property before contacting CashAsIs.

Go through the belongings that matter to you and your family. Take the things you want to keep.

If there are remaining contents, tell us about them when we review the property.

Our goal is to make the property sale simpler—not require your family to spend weeks hauling things away before we will even make an offer.

## No repairs

Inherited houses are often dated or need work.

Maybe the owner lived there for decades. Maybe maintenance was deferred. Maybe the property has been vacant.

You do not need to:

- replace flooring;
- paint;
- remodel the kitchen;
- repair an old bathroom;
- update fixtures;
- landscape the property;
- stage rooms; or
- make the house look ready for buyers.

**CashAsIs evaluates the property in its current condition.**

## No traditional sale preparation

A traditional market sale can involve contractors, cleaning crews, photography, staging, showings, inspections, repair negotiations, and waiting for a buyer's financing.

That's a lot to coordinate when your family is already handling an estate.

CashAsIs purchases directly.

You can show us the property as it sits and get a real purchase number without first spending money preparing it for the market.

## Especially helpful when heirs live somewhere else

Cleaning out an inherited property can be even harder when family members live in different cities or states.

Someone has to travel, coordinate access, hire vendors, decide what stays, and manage the work.

Selling as-is can remove much of that burden.

CashAsIs can work with the family and title while the property remains in its current condition.

## Keep what matters

Selling as-is does **not** mean you have to abandon personal items you care about.

Before closing, identify the belongings your family wants to keep and make arrangements for those items.

Then discuss the remaining contents with us so everyone understands what will stay with the property.

## Start with the offer

Before paying for dumpsters, contractors, repairs, or weeks of cleanup, find out what CashAsIs will pay for the property **as it sits today**.

Send us the property address and tell us a little about the condition.

We'll review it and give you a no-obligation CashAsIs offer.

**Take what you want. Let us help with the property. Move forward without turning an inheritance into another renovation and cleanout project.**

> CashAsIs purchases properties as-is, but what personal property or contents may remain at closing should be agreed upon for the specific transaction. Title and estate requirements vary by property.

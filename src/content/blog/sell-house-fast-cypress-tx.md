## We buy houses in Cypress, TX: 77429 and 77433

Cypress is not a city. It is unincorporated northwest Harris County, so there is no city government and no city property tax. Water and sewer usually come from a municipal utility district (MUD), fire and EMS from a Harris County emergency services district, and the house is valued by the Harris Central Appraisal District. Your tax bill can still be high, because MUD taxes are added to county and school taxes.

We review houses across both Cypress ZIP codes, 77429 on the east side and 77433 on the west side, including master-planned communities such as Bridgeland, Towne Lake, Fairfield, Coles Crossing, and Cypress Creek Lakes, and older neighborhoods along US 290, Spring Cypress Road, and Fry Road.

## Behind on payments? Fort Bend County foreclosure basics

Fort Bend County foreclosure sales are held on the first Tuesday of the month between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. (the first Wednesday if that Tuesday falls on January 1 or July 4). Since December 2, 2025, the county-designated location has been the [Gus George Law Enforcement Academy, Patton Hall, in Richmond](https://www.fortbendcountytx.gov/government/departments/county-clerk/search-for-foreclosures). Foreclosure notices are filed with the County Clerk 21 days before the sale.

If you are behind and considering a sale, start early. Read [how to stop foreclosure in the Houston area](/blog/stop-foreclosure-houston/) for your options.

## The short answer

**Yes. CashAsIs can evaluate a fire-damaged Houston property in its present condition rather than requiring restoration before an offer.**

Fire losses vary widely. One property may have localized damage; another may involve smoke, water from firefighting, electrical concerns, structural damage, boarded openings, or unsafe areas. Give us the history you know and any safe photos, contractor information, adjuster documentation, or reports already available.

Do not enter an unsafe building simply to document it. The goal is to understand the real repair scope, not create another risk. For the broader transaction topic, see [Sell a Fire-Damaged House in Houston](/blog/sell-fire-damaged-house-houston/).

## Fire damage is more than what you can see from the street

A burned area may be only one part of the repair scope. Smoke can affect finishes and contents; firefighting can introduce water; electrical components may need review; openings may need to be secured; and some rooms may be unsafe to enter. Tell us what happened, which areas were affected, whether utilities are on, and whether a contractor, adjuster, engineer, or fire department has produced information you can share. Do not enter an unsafe structure just to take photos for us.

## Insurance and the property sale are separate decisions

If an insurance claim exists, keep the claim documents and tell us its status. A seller should understand any obligations connected with insurance proceeds, lenders, repairs, or closing before assuming how a sale will interact with the claim. CashAsIs can focus on evaluating the real estate in its current condition while the seller, insurer, lender, title company, and appropriate professionals handle their respective parts of the transaction.

## Listing starts a marketing process. CashAsIs starts a sale.

There are two very different ways to sell a property.

With a traditional listing, the first goal is to **put the property on the market and find a buyer**.

With CashAsIs, **you already have the buyer**.

That one difference changes almost everything that happens next.

A listing can be the right tool for an owner who wants to prepare the property, market it publicly, coordinate access, wait for buyer interest and work through the traditional retail-sale process.

But if your goal is simply to **sell the property and move forward**, ask yourself how much of that process you actually want.

## The traditional listing path

A typical listing can involve some version of:

**Prepare → Price → Photograph → Market → Show → Wait → Negotiate → Inspect → Finance → Close**

Every property and transaction is different, but the structure is familiar.

Before the property is sold, someone still has to buy it.

That means getting the property in front of buyers and waiting for one whose price, terms, financing and timeline work for the seller.

For some owners, that's fine.

For others, it feels like turning a property they already want to sell into another project.

## The CashAsIs path

Our process is built differently:

**Property → Cash Offer → Agreement → Title → Closing**

We aren't advertising your property hoping somebody eventually buys it.

**CashAsIs is making the offer to buy it.**

That lets us remove an entire layer of the traditional selling process.

## Listing: get the property ready for the market

A retail buyer may care about first impressions.

That can lead sellers to think about paint, flooring, landscaping, cleaning, decluttering, repairs, photographs and presentation before the listing ever goes live.

Even relatively minor preparation takes time, coordination and sometimes money.

CashAsIs doesn't need your property transformed into a retail product first.

**We want to evaluate the property you actually own today.**

Old kitchen? Tell us.

Roof nearing the end of its life? Tell us.

Needs a cleanout? Tell us.

Deferred maintenance? Tell us.

The condition becomes part of our offer—not a pre-sale assignment for you.

## Listing: keep the property ready for strangers to see

Once listed, the property needs buyer exposure.

That can mean showing appointments, open houses, keeping rooms presentable, coordinating with occupants or tenants, leaving the property during appointments, and adjusting schedules when buyers want access.

And a showing is still only a showing.

It may create an offer.

It may not.

With CashAsIs there is no public marketing campaign and no stream of retail buyers touring the property.

**We're the buyer.**

## Listing: wait for the right buyer

Putting a property on the market doesn't sell it by itself.

The seller still needs an acceptable buyer.

That buyer needs acceptable terms.

If financing is involved, the buyer generally needs to make it through the lender's process as well.

CashAsIs removes the buyer-search stage.

You aren't waiting for us to locate somebody else to purchase the property from you.

We make our offer directly.

## Listing: the negotiation may not end with the accepted offer

A retail buyer may inspect the property after going under contract.

That can create another round of conversations about repairs, credits, concessions or price depending on the contract and what the inspection finds.

Not every buyer asks for changes, and sellers can negotiate or decline requests.

But it's another moving part.

Our CashAsIs offer is made around purchasing the property **as-is**.

The goal isn't to agree to buy the property and then hand you a renovation list.

## Listing: financing can become part of your timeline

A financed retail transaction can involve underwriting, lender documentation, appraisal requirements and loan conditions before closing.

Many financed sales close successfully.

But the seller's closing is still connected to the buyer's financing process.

CashAsIs is structured as a cash purchase.

That means you aren't waiting for a traditional retail buyer to qualify for a mortgage to buy the property from you.

## Listing: selling expenses can stack up

A traditional transaction can include negotiated agent compensation, repairs or preparation, seller closing expenses, concessions and carrying costs depending on the deal.

Those costs vary significantly.

Our separate guide, [What Can You Skip When You Sell Cash As-Is?](/blog/what-can-you-skip-selling-cash-as-is/), breaks down those categories—including a familiar 6% commission example—so sellers can compare the full path rather than only the headline number.

With CashAsIs:

- no real estate commission charged by CashAsIs;
- no repairs required for us;
- no public showings;
- no retail-buyer marketing campaign;
- no traditional buyer inspection/repair negotiation;
- no waiting for a traditional buyer's mortgage; and
- CashAsIs handles the closing costs agreed to in our purchase agreement.

Property-specific obligations such as mortgages, liens or delinquent taxes may still need to be resolved through closing.

## What if the property has a problem?

That's exactly why we don't think every property needs to be forced through the same selling process.

Maybe it's inherited.

Maybe it's vacant.

Maybe it needs repairs.

Maybe you're behind on property taxes.

Maybe a foreclosure date is approaching.

Maybe tenants are involved.

Maybe it already sat on the market and didn't sell.

Or maybe there is no major problem at all—you simply don't want the traditional process.

**Any condition. Just about any situation.**

Start with the property as it sits.

CashAsIs is designed to **buy your property directly**.

If you want the listing process, it exists for a reason.

But if you already know you want to sell and would rather skip preparing, marketing, showing and waiting for a retail buyer, why start with a buyer search when you can start with an offer?

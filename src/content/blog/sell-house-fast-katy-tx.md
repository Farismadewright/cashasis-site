## We buy houses in Katy, TX, in Harris, Fort Bend, and Waller County

A Katy address can sit in any of three counties. Many neighborhoods north of I-10 along Fry Road and Mason Road are in Harris County, much of Cinco Ranch and Grand Lakes is on the Fort Bend County side, and some addresses to the west and northwest fall in Waller County. The county decides which appraisal district values the house (Harris Central, Fort Bend Central, or Waller County) and which tax office the title company checks at closing.

We review houses across the main Katy ZIP codes, 77449, 77450, 77493, and 77494, from older homes near Old Katy to newer master-planned subdivisions. Most of those subdivisions have an HOA, and many sit inside a municipal utility district (MUD), so the tax bill can include MUD taxes on top of county and school taxes.

Parts of the Katy area sit near the Addicks and Barker reservoirs, and some nearby neighborhoods flooded during Hurricane Harvey in 2017. If your house has flood history, tell us up front. A flood map and a house's actual flood history are not the same thing, as our guide to [flood maps vs. flood history](/blog/houston-flood-map-vs-house-flood-history/) explains.

To get started, it helps to know:

- The address and which county it is in, if you know.
- The HOA name and whether any HOA balance or violation is open.
- Known issues such as roof age, foundation movement, plumbing, HVAC, or past water intrusion.
- Whether the house is occupied, vacant, or rented.
- Who is on the deed and roughly what is owed on any mortgage.

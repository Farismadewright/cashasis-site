## Vacant Montgomery County properties can vary widely

A vacant property may be a subdivision house, an older rural home, or a property on a larger lot. Vacancy also means condition can change while the owner is away. CashAsIs evaluates the actual address and property rather than assuming every vacant house has the same repair profile.

Tell us how long it has been vacant, whether utilities are on, what you know about access and condition, and whether anyone is maintaining the property.

## Vacancy changes what you should inspect first

When nobody lives at the property, check for active leaks, broken windows, open doors, storm damage, pests, vegetation against the structure, signs of unauthorized entry, and whether utilities are safely managed. A house that looked fine months ago may have changed while unattended. Do not enter if the structure appears unsafe; photos from accessible areas and a description of what you know are enough to begin a CashAsIs review.

## Larger lots and rural-style properties need address-specific evaluation

Montgomery County properties can differ substantially in lot size, access, improvements, utilities, outbuildings, and neighborhood context. That is why a generic vacant-house formula is not useful. Tell us about acreage, access, any additional structures, well or septic information if applicable, and known restrictions or condition issues. We evaluate the actual parcel and improvements rather than assuming every vacant property fits the same profile.

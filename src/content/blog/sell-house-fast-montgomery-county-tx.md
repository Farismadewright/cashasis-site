## Montgomery County foreclosure sales: when and where

Montgomery County foreclosure sales are held on the first Tuesday of the month between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., on the steps of the Old 1936 Courthouse at 301 N. Main St. in Conroe, the location the Commissioners Court designated on June 14, 2022. Notices must be posted 21 days before the sale, and copies are kept at the [Montgomery County Clerk's office](https://www.mctx.org/public_records/foreclosures/index.php) at 210 W. Davis in Conroe.

Your own notice of sale controls the date, time, and place. If you are behind on payments, read [how to stop foreclosure in the Houston area](/blog/stop-foreclosure-houston/) and our [Montgomery County pre-foreclosure guide](/blog/sell-preforeclosure-house-montgomery-county/).

CashAsIs is a property buyer, not a lender, law firm, or foreclosure-relief service, and we cannot guarantee that a foreclosure will be postponed or stopped.

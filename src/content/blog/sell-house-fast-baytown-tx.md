Most of Baytown, including the 77520 and 77521 ZIP codes, is in Harris County. The east side across Cedar Bayou, mostly ZIP 77523 toward Mont Belvieu, is largely in Chambers County. The county decides which appraisal district values the house and which tax office records the title company checks, so share the full address.

We review houses across Baytown, from older neighborhoods near Texas Avenue and the original downtown to subdivisions off Garth Road, I-10, and SH 146. Many Baytown houses are older and long-owned, which often means original plumbing, older electrical, or a roof that has been through several storms.

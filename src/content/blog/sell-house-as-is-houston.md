## What selling a house as-is in Houston actually means

Selling as-is means the buyer agrees to take the house in its current condition. With a CashAsIs purchase, you do not repair, renovate, stage, or clean out the house for us first, and our offer accounts for the condition we see and what you tell us.

Selling as-is does **not** mean:

- **Hiding known problems.** Texas Property Code Section 5.008 generally requires a seller of a single-family home to give the buyer a written seller's disclosure notice, with some exemptions. An as-is clause does not by itself remove that duty.
- **Skipping title work.** Liens, unpaid property taxes, HOA balances, and every owner's or heir's signature still have to be handled, usually by the title company at closing.
- **Giving up the right to decide.** You can review the offer and the purchase agreement, ask questions, and say no.

If you want the broader idea behind the name, read [selling as-is: what it actually means](/blog/selling-as-is-what-it-actually-means/).

## As-is to a cash buyer vs. as-is on the open market

You can sell a Houston house as-is in two main ways, and either can be the right choice:

- **List it as-is with an agent.** You can reach more buyers. Buyers still usually inspect and may ask for repairs or a lower price, and some loan types require certain repairs before the lender will fund. Commissions, closing costs, and months of holding costs come out of your proceeds.
- **Sell it directly to a cash buyer like CashAsIs.** There are no showings, and the sale does not depend on a retail buyer's mortgage approval. The offer reflects the house's condition, so it can be lower than a retail price for a house in top shape.

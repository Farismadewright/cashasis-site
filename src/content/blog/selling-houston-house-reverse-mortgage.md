**A reverse mortgage does not automatically mean you cannot sell your Houston house. It does mean the loan's repayment requirements need to be part of the sale plan from the beginning.** Identify the loan type, obtain the servicer's current instructions and compare the proposed price with a dated payoff and actual selling expenses.

A buyer saying “we can buy as-is” answers a property-condition question. It does not establish how your loan will be satisfied, who can sign or how much money you will receive. Keep those questions separate so that a convenient sale plan is also a workable closing plan.

## Identify the loan before applying a general rule

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's [reverse-mortgage sale guidance](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-happens-if-i-have-reverse-mortgage-and-i-want-sell-my-home-en-2095/) explains that a sale requires repayment of borrowed money, interest and fees. That page specifically addresses Home Equity Conversion Mortgages, or HECMs. Do not assume every privately offered reverse-mortgage product has identical conditions.

Find the current servicer's name on your loan correspondence and ask it to identify the product and the information needed for a proposed sale. Use an independently verified contact channel. Avoid sending account numbers, identification or complete financial documents through a public comment or an unverified buyer message.

If you are helping a family member, clarify your authority before requesting or signing documents. A willing relative is not automatically an authorized signer. For an inherited-property situation, our [Houston inherited-house guide](/blog/sell-inherited-house-houston/) explains why the ownership and closing process needs its own review.

## Request a payoff for the expected transaction

A monthly statement is useful background, but it is not a substitute for the amount required to satisfy the loan on a particular date. The CFPB's [payoff-versus-balance explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-payoff-amount-and-is-it-the-same-as-my-current-balance-en-205/) distinguishes the current balance from repayment including applicable accrued amounts.

Ask the servicer and closing team to coordinate the actual payoff request. Confirm the date through which it is valid, how a changed closing date is handled, what documents are required and how satisfaction or release is confirmed. Do not calculate an unofficial discount by subtracting a guessed fee from the statement.

Keep one dated working summary rather than several conflicting screenshots. A useful summary identifies the source of each figure, the date obtained, the person handling the next step and any unresolved requirement. Store sensitive documents securely; the summary does not need to circulate publicly.

## Treat notices as a separate action item

The CFPB's [reverse-mortgage resource center](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/reverse-mortgages/) explains that repayment can arise from a sale, a change in principal residence or certain unmet obligations. If you have received a notice, bring the actual document to the servicer and appropriate advisers promptly. This guide cannot determine a private deadline or whether a particular protection applies.

Do not assume a signed purchase agreement stops servicing action, extends a notice period or guarantees that title can close. Ask what must happen next, who is handling it and what evidence confirms completion. If the proposed closing date changes, revisit the loan and occupancy plan instead of relying on the original estimate.

## Do not guess at a shortfall rule

CFPB's HECM sale guidance discusses specific situations involving appraised value, mortgage insurance and a due-and-payable loan. Those conditions are not permission for any buyer to pay any amount and treat the debt as satisfied. If expected proceeds are below the requested payoff, obtain the servicer's instructions and appropriate professional advice before relying on the proposal.

Ask for clear written confirmation of required approvals. A verbal buyer assurance, county tax value or online estimate does not establish lender acceptance. Cash As-Is cannot promise a loan reduction or erase a servicing requirement.

## Compare choices using the money and work remaining

Here is a hypothetical decision, not a claim about local prices: one owner receives a current-condition proposal and an agent's suggested listing plan. The listing plan assumes repairs and a longer preparation period. Compare the same dated loan information, then account for each route's actual preparation, carrying costs, expenses, contingencies and timing. Do not treat the suggested listing price as an already completed sale.
